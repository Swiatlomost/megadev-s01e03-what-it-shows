Two demonstrated mechanisms

Parser: the shared parser always reads month first, even for a day-first tenant. An intended 7 April becomes 4 July. App and email reports are affected; form reports also belong here unless that tenant runs booking-v3’s month-first hint. R-41200 and R-41205 show this path. Morrow Tax uses fr-FR and booking-v2 even though R-41205’s browser is en-US and intake tagged it hint.

Hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY while the tenant’s form interprets input day first. Following the displayed instructions produces the wrong date. Correct the hint to match the form’s parsing contract, or make both honor the same explicit format. This is not fixed by only changing the shared invite parser.

Compute the split from unique IDs, tenant locale and form version, and channel. Use Intl.DateTimeFormat for date order; do not hard-code a list of “EU” locales. The component below uses the unmodified exported classifier and tenant directory.

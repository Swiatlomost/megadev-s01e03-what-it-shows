Context

The subject-only rule in decision 0001 merged different mechanisms into #4471. It also separates identical causes described with different words. The current runnable DedupeExplorer and CauseTable disagree with the old prose.

Decision

This record supersedes 0001 for current triage. Preserve 0001 unedited as an audit trail. Merge only after establishing the same cause in the relevant tenant configuration and channel. For this export, use causeOf and sameCause in src/lib/kb/causes.ts with tenantOf in locales.ts. A matching subject is a search clue, not proof. Unknown tenants or uncertain mechanisms stay separate pending investigation; two unknown causes do not make a duplicate.

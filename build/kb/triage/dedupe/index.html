Triage: duplicates share a demonstrated cause

owner Swiatlomost with AI harness current reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Current rule

This guide supersedes the old subject-only procedure, which followed decision 0001, reviewed in June 2024. That decision is preserved as history; decision 0002 records the correction.

  1. Keep each incoming report and its ID. Deduplicate export overlap by ID, not by subject.
  2. Read the body and identify tenant, locale, form version, channel and expected versus actual behavior.
  3. Compare the demonstrated cause with an open issue. For this date incident run causeOf and sameCause.
  4. Merge only when causes match. Matching subjects alone are insufficient.
  5. If tenant or cause is unknown, keep it separate and ask for reproduction evidence.
  6. Keep reporting and classification uncertainties visible. Do not force anomalous symptoms into a confident diagnosis.

Same tenant with app and email may share a cause even when the words differ. The same tenant with form and app may have different causes despite identical words. Different tenants can have the same cause; always check configuration.

Freshness

Read the signed #4471 review and check the current queue before acting. Receipt time measures when a report arrived; merge time measures when the desk grouped it. Do not close an issue merely because the merged parent says “known”.
